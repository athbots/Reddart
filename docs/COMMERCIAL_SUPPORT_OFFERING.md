## 1. Philosophy: Open Core Engine, Sovereign Paid Support

Reddart's core editing engine is and will remain **permanently free, open-source (MIT OR Apache-2.0), and 100% offline with zero subscriptions**. 

However, commercial broadcast studios, film production companies, and high-volume corporate media teams require guaranteed SLAs, priority bug remediation, custom codec integrations, and turnkey hardware certification. Reddart offers three distinct commercial support and deployment tiers:

## 2. Commercial Support Tiers

### Tier 1: Creator Pro ($29/month or $290/year per creator)
- **Target:** Independent professional YouTubers, boutique commercial videographers, and freelance colorists.
- **Benefits:**
  - Guaranteed 24-hour response SLA on technical bug reports and timeline recovery requests.
  - Custom calibrated export preset creation (tailored to specific client delivery standards).
  - Priority access to closed-alpha releases and experimental AI neural features (Whisper auto-captions, spectral voice isolation).
  - Monthly asynchronous workflow review with Reddart systems engineers.

### Tier 2: Studio Team ($199/month or $1,990/year for up to 10 seats)
- **Target:** Digital marketing agencies, corporate media divisions, and post-production houses.
- **Benefits:**
  - 4-hour critical issue response SLA with prioritized code patches.
  - Dedicated Shared Storage (NAS/SAN) optimization for multi-editor media indexing.
  - Direct Slack/Discord channel access to Reddart core developers.
  - Quarterly remote workflow audits and timeline performance profiling.
  - Assistance with custom `.cube` 3D LUT look generation and corporate brand templates.

### Tier 3: Enterprise & Broadcast Studio (Custom Annual Contract)
- **Target:** Film studios, broadcast television networks, defense contractors, and air-gapped forensic labs.
- **Benefits:**
  - 1-hour critical response SLA with 24/7 emergency incident coverage.
  - Complete air-gapped installation verification (zero outbound network packets guaranteed by cryptographic audit).
  - Custom hardware encoder pipeline development (ProRes 4444 XQ, DNxHR, SMPTE-2110 IP video bridge).
  - Custom turnkey workstation certification (benchmarked and calibrated hardware procurement).
  - Indemnification and dedicated long-term support (LTS) release branches.

## 3. Pilot Program: The Closed-Alpha Support Test

To establish initial market willingness to pay and validate demand for professional support:
1. **Pilot Cohort:** 10 commercial production studios recruited from the closed-alpha pool will receive complimentary 60-day access to Tier 2 Studio Team support.
2. **Evaluation Metrics:**
   - Ticket volume per seat per month.
   - Percentage of tickets involving codec/hardware driver anomalies vs editing feature requests.
   - Willingness to convert to a paid annual SLA contract at the end of the pilot period.
3. **Inquiries:** Contact `partners@reddart.org` for commercial support pilot onboarding.
